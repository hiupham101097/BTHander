# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Khach hang ca nhan va doanh nghiep dang tim doi tac de xay dung phan mem, mobile app, website, game hoac giai phap thiet ke may.
- Nguoi dung da dang ky can truy cap ho so ca nhan, theo doi va the hien su quan tam den du an.
- Thanh vien noi bo quan ly noi dung, du an, nguoi dung va lien he qua khu vuc quan tri rieng.

## Product Purpose

BThander gioi thieu nang luc ket hop phat trien phan mem va ky thuat che tao, cho phep khach hang xem du an, dich vu, cong cu mien phi, doi ngu va gui yeu cau hop tac. Thanh cong la khi khach truy cap nhanh chong hieu BThander lam gi, tin vao nang luc thuc thi va co mot duong hanh dong ro rang de bat dau trao doi.

## Positioning

BThander ket hop ky nang xay dung san pham so voi tu duy thiet ke va che tao ky thuat trong cung mot doi ngu, thay vi chi cung cap mot nhom dich vu phan mem don le.

## Operating Context

Khach hang danh gia BThander qua trang chu, case study du an, ho so thanh vien, bai viet va cac dich vu. Ho co the dang ky, dang nhap, quan tam den du an va gui bieu mau lien he. Noi dung cong khai duoc cap nhat tu API va co cac trang thai dang tai, rong va loi.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 18, Vite va React Router; deploy tren Cloudflare Workers voi API va D1.
- Giu nguyen cau truc route, schema form, hanh vi xac thuc, API va cac luong quan tri hien co.
- Pham vi redesign nay la cac surface public va auth; khu vuc admin van la mot product surface rieng.
- Noi dung chinh bang tieng Viet va phai hoat dong tot tren desktop lan mobile.

## Brand Commitments

- Ten va wordmark BThander / Brave Trust Hander duoc giu nguyen.
- Logo hien co tai `public/images/bthander-logo-mark.png` la tai san nhan dien chinh.
- Giong noi can truc tiep, co nang luc ky thuat, dang tin va tranh phong dai.
- Huong hinh anh da duoc nguoi dung yeu cau: hien dai, thu hut, mang hoi huong cong nghe.

## Evidence on Hand

- Anh hero va anh nang luc that dang duoc su dung trong `public/images/`.
- Du lieu du an, san pham, doi ngu va bai viet duoc tai tu API.
- Cac con so thanh tich hien co o `src/constants/data.js`; khong tao them so lieu, khach hang, chung thuc hoac claim moi.

## Product Principles

- Cho thay nang luc qua san pham, hinh anh va case study, khong chi qua khau hieu.
- De hanh dong chinh luon ro rang cho nguoi truy cap moi.
- Giu trai nghiem ky thuat hien dai nhung noi dung va tuong tac van de doc, de hieu.
- Bao toan cac luong dang nhap, lien he va du lieu dang van hanh.
- Thiet ke responsive va ton trong reduced motion, keyboard focus va do tuong phan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Giao dien web can ho tro dieu huong ban phim, focus state ro rang, contrast WCAG AA, noi dung tieng Viet de doc va tat chuyen dong khi nguoi dung yeu cau reduced motion.
